---
title: Why I Keep Coming Back to Processing
date: 2026-05-20
---

Every year I tell myself I will move to something faster, something lower to the metal. And every year I open Processing again because the distance between an idea and a running sketch is so short that I forget I am programming at all.

That immediacy matters more than performance for the way I work. _First Earth_ and _Chrono Convergence_ both started as throwaway sketches I wrote in an afternoon, never expecting them to become anything. The tool that lets you be careless is the tool that lets you be surprised.

So I keep coming back — not because it is the best, but because it gets out of the way.
